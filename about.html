1. MY BACKGROUND AND STORY

▾

My career really began as a copywriter, a natural extension of my university background in English Linguistics & Literature, with a focus on US and UK culture. Words were where I started and they've stayed at the core of everything I do since.

When I joined Launchmen, I came on as a Content Specialist. But in a startup, you rarely stay in one lane. My boss saw potential beyond writing and transitioned me into a Creative Project Manager role, training me to manage, write, and design across the board. That environment taught me more than I could have imagined — how to wear multiple hats, move fast and own a project from concept to delivery.

2. WHAT I VALUE

▾

A good project manager needs three things: to be likable, organized, and just worried enough.

Likable, because projects move through people.

Organized, because someone has to hold the moving parts together.

Worried enough, because a little concern is what catches small problems before they grow.

3. HOW I WORK

▾

I'm decisive, detail-oriented, and responsible.

Decisive, because momentum matters and a project can't wait on indecision.

Detail-oriented, because the small things are usually where quality lives or dies.

And responsible, because when I'm in charge of something, I own the outcome — I don't pass blame for what was mine to carry.

4. WHAT I'M LOOKING FOR

▾

Somewhere I can keep building — skills, knowledge and range. I don't want to be the kind of PM who only communicates and tracks progress. I want to understand how each part of a project actually works: development, editing, writing, design, QC, and everything in between. Enough to step in and handle it myself when someone on the team is out. For me, the goal isn't just to manage the work, it's to truly understand it.
